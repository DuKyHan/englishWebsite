UNIT 2 – DO YOU EVER BINGE-WATCH?

LESSON 2: GRAMMAR
Wh-questions in the Present Simple

I. WH-WORDS

Wh-wordasks aboutNghĩa

II. FORM

1. With “be”

Wh- + am / is / are (not) + S?

What is the TV show? – It's Family Feud.

Who is the TV host? – Steve Harvey is the TV host.

2. With “do / does”

Wh- + do / does (not) + S + V (bare)?

When do you watch Britain's Got Talent? – I watch it at 8:00 pm.

Which channel does he usually watch news on? – He usually watches it on HTV7.

3. Who / What as the subject (no do / does)

Who / What + V-s/es …?

Who hosts the reality show on Friday night?

III. IMPORTANT NOTES

  • I / you / we / they → do · he / she / it → does
  • After do / does → bare verb: Why does he loves love Rap Việt?
  • Make a question for the underlined words → remove the underlined words.
⚠ COMMON MISTAKES
  • Where you watch TV? ❌ → Where do you watch TV? ✓
  • Why do the character loves…? ❌ → Why does the character love…? ✓
  • What does Emily watches? ❌ → What does Emily watch? ✓
⭐ CORE KNOWLEDGE – Nhớ trước khi kiểm tra
  1. Why = reason · Where = place · When = time · Who = people · How = manner
  2. What = something · Which = choice
  3. Wh + be + S? · Wh + do/does + S + V?
  4. does + V bare (no -s).
  5. Who / What is the subject → no do/does: Who hosts…?

1–2. What is it? · Nó là gì?

Wh-questions là câu hỏi bắt đầu bằng từ để hỏi (What, Who, Where…). Câu trả lời không phải Yes / No mà là một thông tin cụ thể.

Yes / No question

Do you watch Rap Việt? – Yes, I do.

Wh-question

When do you watch Rap Việt? – On Saturday.

4. Form

With “be”

Wh- + am / is / are + S?

What is the TV show?
Where are the contestants?

With “do / does”

Wh- + do / does + S + V?

When do you watch it?
Why does he love Rap Việt?

💡 Learn more: Who / What làm chủ ngữ

Khi Who / What hỏi về chủ ngữ, không dùng do/does, động từ thêm -s: Who hosts the show? – A comedian hosts the show. So sánh: Who does Laura watch? (hỏi tân ngữ).

📒 Notebook

7. Step-by-step · Cách làm bài

1
Look at the underlined words. Time → When; place → Where; person → Who; reason (because…) → Why; thing → What; way → How.
2
Find the verb. is/are → move it before the subject. Other verbs → add do / does.
3
Change the verb to the bare form after do/does: watches → watch, loves → love.
4
Remove the underlined words and add “?”.

Wrap up

Chain story

The teacher starts with a simple sentence. Student 1 asks a Wh-question about it. Student 2 answers AND asks a new Wh-question. Keep going around the class in the Present Simple!

💡 Example

Teacher: There is a boy named Alex.
Student 1: Where does Alex live?
Student 2: Alex lives in a big city. What does he like to do?
Student 3: He likes to play soccer. Who is his best friend?
