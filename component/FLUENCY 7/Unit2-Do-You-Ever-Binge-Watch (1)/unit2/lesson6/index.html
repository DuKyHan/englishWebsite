🎯 Learning goals · Mục tiêu

  1. Nhận ra bài review tích cực, tiêu cực hay cả hai.
  2. Dùng However và although để nối ý trái ngược.
  3. Dùng cụm từ nêu ý kiến: I believe that…, I find…, Personally, I think…
  4. Lập dàn ý và viết review 60–80 từ cho một chương trình TV.

LESSON 6: WRITING
Writing reviews for a TV show

I. PHRASES TO GIVE OPINIONS

  • I really think that … · Personally, I think …
  • I'm sure that … · Obviously, it's …
  • I believe that … · I find it …
  • In my opinion, … · From my point of view, …

II. LINKING CONTRASTING IDEAS

…, However, … / S + V, although S + V.

It's educational. However, some questions are too hard.

A few questions are at higher levels, although the kids give the correct answers.

III. ADJECTIVES

amazing, fun, helpful, informative, nice, catchy, educational, entertaining, good, boring, hard, silly, ignorant, hilarious

IV. WHAT TO WRITE IN A TV SHOW REVIEW

  • People: the guests, the participants, the hosts, the audiences
  • Format: the contents, the rules, the prizes
  • Others: the theme music, the lights, the television studios, the sound systems, the stage
⭐ CORE KNOWLEDGE – Nhớ trước khi kiểm tra
  1. However / although → link contrasting ideas.
  2. I think / I believe / I find → writer's opinions.
  3. Give positive AND negative points + examples.
  4. Plan: format → like (why) → dislike (why) → what you learn / ideas to improve → star rating.

Write your TV show review

Useful language · Phrases to give opinions
I really think that …Personally, I think …I'm sure that …Obviously, it's …I believe that …I find it …In my opinion, …From my point of view, …
EXAM ADVICE
• Decide which TV show you are writing about. Make sure you know it well.
• Note the format of the TV show and think of adjectives you want to use to describe it.
• Give positive and negative reviews which help people have a clear understanding (the audiences, the host, the quality of sound systems or lights, contents, the studio, etc.).
• Use phrases or examples which support your opinions.
