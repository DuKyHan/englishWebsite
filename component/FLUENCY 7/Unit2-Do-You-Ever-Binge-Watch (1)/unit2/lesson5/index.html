Talking about a favorite TV show

I. VOCABULARY

    II. USEFUL LANGUAGE

    • Advantages: Watching TV helps teenagers broaden their knowledge / reduce stress / connect family members.
    • Disadvantages: It can make teenagers feel isolated / cause health problems / make them lazy.
    • Giving opinions: I think watching news is informative. It helps me …
    • Presenting a show: It's a … for teenagers. The aim of the show is … The format is … I like it because …

    III. PRONUNCIATION – SENTENCE STRESS

    Usually stressed (content words)Not usually stressed (function words)
    main verbs · nouns · adjectives · Wh-questions · negativesarticles · prepositions · modal verbs · pronouns · conjunctions · helping verbs · quantifiers

    My mom really loves watching cooking shows.

    ⭐ CORE KNOWLEDGE – Nhớ trước khi kiểm tra
    1. Content words (N, V, adj, Wh-, not) → stressed.
    2. Function words (a, the, on, can, him, and, is…) → usually not stressed.
    3. Speak a little slower; give your opinion with an adjective + a reason.
    4. I think watching + N + is + adj. It helps me + V.

    🔊 Pronunciation · Exercises 5–6

    Sentence stress

    Sentence stress refers to the words in a sentence that get the most emphasis.

    Usually stressed (content words)
    • Main verbs
    • Nouns
    • Adjectives
    • Wh-questions
    • Negatives
    Not usually stressed (function words)
    • Articles (a, an, the)
    • Prepositions (for, of, …)
    • Modal verbs (can, may, …)
    • Pronouns (him, her, …)
    • Conjunctions (but, as, …)
    • Helping verbs (is, are, …)
    • Quantifiers (many, few, …)

    My mom really loves watching cooking shows.
