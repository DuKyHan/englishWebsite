    III. USEFUL STRUCTURES

    Have you ever + V3/ed …?

    Have you ever binge-watched a TV show?

    S + be + broadcast on + channel

    The 7th Wish is broadcast on VTV3.

    help + sb + V (bare)

    The production team helps them materialize their dreams.

    make + sb + adj / V

    It made people feel emotional. · The disease made Tan unable to move.

    💡 Learn more: tear-jerker là gì?

    tear (nước mắt) + jerk (giật mạnh) → tear-jerker = phim / chương trình “lấy nước mắt”, làm người xem buồn và khóc nhiều.

    ⭐ CORE KNOWLEDGE – Nhớ trước khi kiểm tra
    1. incidents = unpleasant life events · insightful = deep understanding
    2. materialize = become real · extraordinary = unusual and special
    3. broadcast = show on TV · episodes = parts of the show
    4. The 7th Wish: reality show on VTV3, aims to fulfill dreams of unlucky people.
    5. make + sb + adj: made people feel emotional / made Tan unable to move.
