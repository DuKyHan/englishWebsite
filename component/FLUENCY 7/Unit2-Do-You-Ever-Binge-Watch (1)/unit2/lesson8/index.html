UNIT 2 – DO YOU EVER BINGE-WATCH?

LESSON 8: INTERNATIONAL EXAM
KET & PTE Academic

I. VOCABULARY

    II. EXAM TIPS

    • Listening (pictures): read the question, look at all pictures, listen for the final answer (speakers often correct themselves).
    • Gap fill: one word, a number, a date or a time – check spelling: products, three, streaming, 100,000,000, 30th.
    • Reading (choose the word): check grammar (tense, word form) and meaning.
    • Summary (50–70 words): Who? What? Where? How long? What happened in the end?

    III. GRAMMAR REVIEW

    • Past continuous + past simple: Tom was cleaning the living room when Jane arrived.
    • Present perfect for a past action with a result now / experience: Look! He has broken his arm. Have you ever read…?
    • Past simple with a finished time: Mary took these photos yesterday.
    • Big numbers: 450,000,000 = four hundred and fifty million.
    ⭐ CORE KNOWLEDGE – Nhớ trước khi kiểm tra
    1. Listen to the end – the first number/answer may be a trap.
    2. Word form: collect → collection, educate → education, wonder → wonderful, regular → regularly.
    3. yesterday / last … / ago → past simple; ever / for 5 years (still now) / Look! → present perfect.
