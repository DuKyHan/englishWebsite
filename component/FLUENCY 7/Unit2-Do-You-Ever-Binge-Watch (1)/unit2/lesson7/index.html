Sound waves

I. VOCABULARY

    II. HOW SOUND WAVES TRAVEL

    • Sound is a type of energy made by vibrations.
    • Vibrations create sound waves which travel through air, solids and liquids.
    • Air: a bird sings → sound waves travel through air → our ears.
    • Solids: knock on a wooden door → the wood vibrates → sound waves travel through the door.
    • Liquids: ring a bell in water → water particles vibrate and produce sound waves.
    ⭐ CORE KNOWLEDGE – Nhớ trước khi kiểm tra
    1. No vibration → no sound.
    2. Sound waves travel through air (A), solids (S) and liquids (L).
    3. String telephone: the voice travels through the string (solid); a loose or held string stops the vibration.

    Exercise 4 · Do it!

    Make a string telephone. In pairs, do the project and write a report.

    Materials
    • 2 paper cups (2 cốc giấy)
    • A string (1 sợi dây)
    Instructions
    1. Use a push pin or any objects that you can use to make a hole in the bottom of 2 cups. (Dùng đinh ghim tạo lỗ ở đáy 2 cốc.)
    2. Thread the string through the hole of each cup. (Luồn dây qua lỗ mỗi cốc.)
    3. Tie a paper clip on the end inside the cups. (Buộc kẹp giấy vào đầu dây bên trong cốc.)
    4. Find a friend, talk through the inside of your cup while they listen on their cup. (Nói vào cốc của mình, bạn nghe ở cốc kia.)
