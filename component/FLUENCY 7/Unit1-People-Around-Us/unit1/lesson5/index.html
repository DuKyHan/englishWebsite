UNIT 1 – PEOPLE AROUND US

LESSON 5: SPEAKING
Describing admirable people

I. VOCABULARY

    II. USEFUL LANGUAGE

    1. Introduction

    • I'd like to talk about …
    • Let me tell you about …

    2. Body

    • I first met … when …
    • The reason I admire … is …
    • I admire [person's name] for his / her …

    3. Conclusion

    • In conclusion, I'm truly grateful for …
    • To wrap up, I feel fortunate to have [person's name] as a role model in my life.
    💡 Learn more: whom / who

    “my English teacher whom I really admire” – whom (= người mà) thay cho người làm tân ngữ. Trong văn nói, người ta thường dùng who: “the teacher who I admire”.

    III. PRONUNCIATION – WORD STRESS

    • 2-syllable nouns / adjectives → stress on the 1st syllable: PARent, PERfect
    • 2-syllable verbs → stress on the 2nd syllable: adMIRE
    • Words ending in -ic, -sion, -tion → stress right before the ending: reaLIStic, inVAsion, converSAtion
    ⭐ CORE KNOWLEDGE – Nhớ trước khi kiểm tra
    1. admire (v) → admirable (adj): I admire my teacher. She is admirable.
    2. Bài nói: Introduction → Body (appearance, personality, reason) → Conclusion.
    3. I admire [name] for his / her + noun (guidance, kindness…).
    4. be grateful for / to have · feel fortunate to have … as a role model.
    5. Stress: N/Adj 1st · V 2nd · before -ic / -sion / -tion.

    🔊 Pronunciation · Track 1.05

    Word stress

    Word stress is the emphasis we place on a specific syllable of a word when pronouncing it.

    RuleExamples
    2-syllable nouns, adjectivesstress on the first syllableparent · perfect
    2-syllable verbsstress on the second syllableadmire
    Words end with -ic, -sion, -tionstress next to -ic, -sion, -tionrealistic · invasion · conversation
