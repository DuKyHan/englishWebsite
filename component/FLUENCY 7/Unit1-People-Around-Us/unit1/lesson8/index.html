Exam skills & Unit 1 review

I. NEW WORDS

    II. EXAM TIPS

    1. Reading (KET)

    • Read the question first. Find who wrote the message and why.
    • The answer often uses different words: “forgot my lunch… hope for a better memory” → often forgets important things.

    2. Listening (TOEFL Junior)

    • Main idea questions: “What are the speakers mainly discussing?”
    • Detail questions: listen for names, hair, eyes, personality words.

    3. Speaking (PTE)

    • Read aloud: pause at commas and full stops; stress key words.
    • Repeat sentence: remember the meaning, not single words.

    III. UNIT 1 REVIEW

    • Appearance: has + hair / eyes · Personality: is + adjective
    • Possessives: my – mine, your – yours, her – hers… · Whose … is it?
    • It's very generous of you to help me. · I'm not very good at Math.
    • An introverted person prefers spending time alone.
    ⭐ CORE KNOWLEDGE – Nhớ trước khi kiểm tra
    1. Đọc câu hỏi trước, gạch chân từ khóa.
    2. Đáp án thường là cách nói khác (paraphrase) của bài đọc / nghe.
    3. Whose + N + is it? → It's mine / yours / hers…
    4. be good at · confident in · It's + adj + of you + to V.
    5. blond = pale yellow hair · introverted = prefers being alone.

    Wrap up

    Who is that?

    The teacher randomly invites one student to stand up. The student describes the appearance and personality of a classmate. The rest of the class guesses who it is.

    Appearance

    • This person has … hair (long / short, straight / curly, black / brown).
    • He / She has … eyes. He / She wears glasses.
    • He / She is tall / short.

    Personality

    • He / She is very … (friendly, cheerful, reliable…).
    • He / She is a bit … (shy, talkative…).
    • His / Her favorite subject is …
    💬 ExampleThis person has short black hair and brown eyes. She wears glasses. She is quite shy but extremely helpful. Her favorite subject is Math. Who is she?
