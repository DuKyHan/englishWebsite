      💡 Learn more: Mẹo nhớ bằng tiền tố, hậu tố

      im- = không: patient (kiên nhẫn) → impatient (thiếu kiên nhẫn).
      -less = không có: care → careless (bất cẩn).
      -ful = đầy: cheer → cheerful, help → helpful.
      -ish: self → selfish (chỉ nghĩ đến bản thân).

      II. PHRASAL VERBS

        III. USEFUL STRUCTURES

        S + be / look / seem + (very / so / too) + adj

        Sarah always looks cheerful.

        It is + adj + of + sb + to V

        It is generous of you to donate to the charity.

        too + adj + to V  ·  so + adj + that …

        Alice is too stubborn to apologize. · He is so careless that he makes mistakes.

        be confident in · be impatient with sb

        IV. IMPORTANT NOTES

        • Opposites: patient ≠ impatient · careful ≠ careless · generous ≠ selfish · diligent ≠ lazy
        • Phrasal verbs đổi dạng động từ: take → took after · make → made up with · stand → stood up for · He takes after / looks up to.
        ⚠ COMMON MISTAKES
        • I get along my sister. ❌ → I get along with my sister. ✓
        • She takes after with her mom. ❌ → She takes after her mom. ✓
        • They had a fight, then they took after each other. ❌ → made up with ✓
        • He is patient. He always gets angry. ❌ → He is impatient. ✓
        ⭐ CORE KNOWLEDGE – Nhớ trước khi kiểm tra
        1. Positive: generous, cheerful, diligent, confident, reliable.
        2. Negative: impatient, stubborn, selfish, bossy, careless.
        3. get along with = hòa hợp · make up with = làm lành
        4. look up to = ngưỡng mộ · stand up for = bênh vực · take after = giống (người thân)
        5. Đọc ngữ cảnh (+ hay −) để chọn tính từ đúng.

        Wrap up

        Blankety Blank

        📋 How to play (theo PPT)
        1. Each round needs 2 players and 6 panelists.
        2. The teacher shows the players a question such as “Anna is a great student as she's always _____”.
        3. 6 panelists write their answers on pieces of paper. DO NOT SHOW THEM UNTIL THE TEACHER ASKS.
        4. 2 players then write their answers on the board.
        5. If the answer of the player is the same as one of any panelists, the player gets points, equal to the number of panelists that have the same answer.
        6. The winner is the person who has the most points. Then change the players and continue the game.
