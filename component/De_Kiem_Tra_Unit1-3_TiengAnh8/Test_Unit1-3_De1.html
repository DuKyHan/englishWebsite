📖 Bài đọc — Unit 1: Two Friends, Two Different Hobbies

Trang loves doing DIY activities in her free time. She often spends hours knitting, building dollhouses, or making paper flowers. She says she prefers doing these things alone because they help her relax. Her friend Tom, however, is keen on going out. He likes hanging out with friends, going to the cinema, cycling, and playing sport. One Sunday, Tom invited Trang to watch a comedy at New World Cinema with him and Mark. Trang agreed and asked if she could bring her friend Mai. They agreed to meet outside the cinema at 9 a.m.

📖 Bài đọc — Unit 2: A Memorable Summer in the Countryside

Last summer, Mai spent her holiday at her uncle's house in a small village in Bac Ninh province. It was harvest time, so she helped the farmers load rice onto a truck and dry it in the sun. In the afternoons, she herded buffaloes and cows with the village children. In the evenings, they flew kites over the paddy fields and played traditional games together. Mai said life in the countryside was quieter than in the city, but it felt healthier and more relaxing.
