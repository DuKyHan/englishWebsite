📖 Bài đọc — Unit 1: A Popular Hobby among Teens

Knitting is becoming a popular hobby among teenagers again. Many young people learn to knit by watching online videos. It is a relaxing activity that helps reduce stress and can also save money because you can make your own clothes, scarves, or bags. However, knitting also requires patience because a simple scarf can take several days to finish. Some students even sell the things they knit to earn extra pocket money.

📖 Bài đọc — Unit 3: A Student Becomes Addicted to His Phone

Nam used to spend only a little time on his phone, but things changed after he got his own smartphone account. He started to check notifications every few minutes, browse social media for hours, and upload posts about his daily life. He even stayed up late logging on to forums to chat with online friends. As a result, he found it hard to concentrate on his schoolwork and became less focused in class. His parents were worried about him, so they helped him set a daily time limit for using his phone and encouraged him to spend more time on offline hobbies instead.
