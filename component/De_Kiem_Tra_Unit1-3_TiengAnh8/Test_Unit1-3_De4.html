📖 Bài đọc — Unit 3: A Class Dealing with Stress and New Clubs

Minh says he feels pressure not only from his studies but also from his parents and friends. His teacher advises the class to stay calm, work hard, and use the new Facebook group to discuss any problems. Ann, another student, says the Facebook group is very user-friendly. She is excited about joining the new arts-and-crafts club and the music club, which both offer a variety of activities and regular competitions.
