📖 Bài đọc — Unit 2: Life in a Picturesque Village

Many tourists enjoy visiting the countryside because it is picturesque and peaceful. Local people there are usually very hospitable; they often invite visitors into their homes and share their regional specialities. Some villages near the sea have old lighthouses that guide fishing boats home at night. In the countryside, families often grow fruit trees in their orchards and raise cattle for milk and meat. Although life there can be less convenient than in the city because of the lack of facilities such as hospitals or shopping malls, many people believe it is a more peaceful place to live.

📖 Bài đọc — Unit 3: A Class Dealing with Stress and New Clubs

His teacher advises the class to stay calm, work hard, and use the new Facebook group to discuss any problems. Ann, another student, says the Facebook group is very user-friendly. She is excited about joining the new arts-and-crafts club and the music club, which both offer a variety of activities and regular competitions.
